## What the conservative rule blocks (qualitative)

105 fewer baskets traded vs the aggressive config. The selectivity rules out:

1. **3-arm "agreement" setups where one indicator was just past threshold** — these are the borderline configurations Min_Conf=4 is designed to filter. In 2024, blocking them prevented the Sep 11 SHORT emergency.
2. **Baskets that would have stacked to L5 or L6** before recovering — without the deeper grid coverage, the same setups that would have gone deep just don't open the L1.
3. **Probes during ambiguous mean-reversion zones** — small wins that would have come from quick L1-only TP closes.

The lost upside in 2025 (−$176) is mostly category 3 — small wins on baskets that would have closed cleanly at L1 or L2. The structural protection is paid for by these.

## Total exposure check at the deepest 2025 basket

The 4 L4 baskets accumulated up to: `0.10 + 2.40 + 3.60 + 4.80 = 10.90 lots` (L4 sum). At 30-pip step, L4 sits 90 pips below L1.

For a hypothetical 200-pip continuation below L4:
- Total adverse from L1: 290 pips
- wavg displacement from L1: 0.10×0 + 2.40×(-30) + 3.60×(-60) + 4.80×(-90) = -720 pip-lots / 10.90 = **−66 pips below L1**
- Distance from wavg at the bottom: 290 - 66 = 224 pips below wavg
- Floating loss: `10.90 × 0.0731 × 224 ≈ $178`
- DD on $2k: **8.9%** — well under 35%

Even if 2025 had a worst-case 200-pip continuation past L4 (which didn't actually happen — max was 90 pips), the cap stays unreachable. The configuration is structurally safe.

**Pending 2023 confirmation, this is the recommended production setup.**
