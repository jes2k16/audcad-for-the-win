## What did the input changes actually buy us?

vs the prior v1.5 July 2024 run ($1k, default 22-pip grid, auto-sized):

| Lever | Value in $1k Jul'24 run | Value in $2k 2025 run | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting capital | $1,000 | **$2,000** | doubled the buffer above the $963 vol_min freeze floor — turns a 3.7%-of-equity blast radius into a 51.9% one |
| Grid step | 22 pips | **30 pips** | wider spacing → fewer legs per 100-pip adverse move (~3 vs ~5) |
| Lot sizing | auto (would be 0.20+ at $2k) | **fixed 0.10** | manual override caps exposure — auto would have scaled the base up and partially canceled the grid widening |
| `wc_pct` at probe-open | 32.62% | **20.73%** | 12pp of pre-baked safety margin |

The math from the prior reply: with these inputs the July 2024 178-pip drop would have stacked ~6 legs (24.10 lots), wavg displaced ~−105 pips, floating loss of ~$129 at the bottom = **6.5% DD** — nowhere near the 35% emergency cap.

The 2025 deepest basket (Sep 12 → 24) confirmed exactly that: ~109-pip adverse, 6 legs, 24.10 lots, and price reverted within 2 more days. The trap that froze the $1k account in 2024 simply doesn't fire at this configuration.

## Caveat — this is 2025, not 2024

2025 was the gentlest year in the test set (v1.4 cent $1k auto-sized also returned +107% in 2025). The brutal years for AUDCAD were 2023 and 2024. **Until this configuration is tested on those years, declare victory only partially.**

Specifically, the structural questions still open:

1. **Replay 2024 with this exact setup** ($2k, fixed 0.10, step=30) — predicted to survive based on the math, but unconfirmed.
2. **Replay 2023 with same setup** — 2023 had two L8 emergencies in June ($1k v1.4 run lost 16.2%). Wider grid should help, but capital headroom is the dominant variable.
3. **What happens with $1k + this setup?** — the fixed 0.10 lot at $1k with `wc_pct ≈ 41%` will trigger the FitCheck. So this combo is genuinely $2k-only.

## Verdict

For 2025: **the configuration works**. +15.66%, 95.7% win rate, zero emergencies, max 6 legs reached. Boring in the best possible way.

The wins are smaller than the more aggressive auto-sized v1.4 run (+107% on $1k 2025), but the trade-off is real safety margin. The $1k auto config was a coin flip year-to-year between huge gains (2025) and account freeze (2023, 2024). This setup gives up some upside in exchange for actually surviving the bad years — pending confirmation on 2024 and 2023 replays.

**Next step**: re-run the same inputs ($2k, fixed 0.10, step=30, Min_Confluence=3, gate ON) on the full 2024 and 2023 datasets. Those are the real validation gates.
