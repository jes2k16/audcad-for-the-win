## What EMA200 Did Differently

### It permanently blocked almost all SHORT entries

D1 EMA200 in mid-2024 reflects AUDCAD's price over the prior 200 daily closes (~2023 and early 2024), roughly anchored around **0.89–0.90**. Throughout most of 2024, AUDCAD price traded **above** this level — which the gate interprets as "long-term trend bullish, do not short."

Result: **1,371 GATE_BLOCK SHORT** events vs only **523 GATE_BLOCK LONG** — the EMA200 was running AUDCAD as a "longs only" strategy for virtually the entire year.

### It blocked the fatal Sep 11 SHORT at the exact bar

The Sep 11 23:15 SHORT entry at 0.90555 — which opened the fatal basket in v1/v2 (resulting in L8 emergency Sep 27) — was **GATE_BLOCK | SHORT | htf_veto_sell**:

```
Sep 11 07:00   PROBE_OPEN | LONG  @ 0.90505  gate_l=1 gate_s=0   ← long OK
Sep 11 23:15   GATE_BLOCK | SHORT @ 0.90555  htf_veto_sell        ← short KILLED ✅
```

With EMA200 sitting below current price, the gate correctly said "this is a bullish long-term environment — don't short." That single block eliminated the entire Sep 27 emergency.

### It did NOT block the July LONG — and couldn't

The Jul 18 10:30 LONG at 0.92130 passed gate_l=1 because AUDCAD was above EMA200. The EMA200 was correct by its own logic — AUDCAD *was* in a long-term bullish trend. The problem is that a legitimately bullish long-term trend can still have a violent 319-pip 3-week correction. EMA200 has no mechanism to detect that.

## Why the Account Survived But Ended Negative

### The EMA200 prevented the second emergency → no Q4 freeze

In v1/v2 (EMA20):
1. Aug 5 emergency → equity drops to ~$1,400
2. Sep 27 emergency → equity drops to ~$1,200 → fit_check_fail → **frozen Oct–Dec**

In v3 (EMA200):
1. Aug 5 emergency → equity drops to ~$1,409
2. Sep was **longs only** — 21 probes all LONG, all closed at TP
3. Oct–Dec traded normally — 17+15+26 = 58 more probes, all closed at TP
4. Result: **0 SKIP_PROBE, active through Dec 30**

### But recovery at 0.10 lot is extremely slow

Each L1-only basket earns: `0.10 lots × 0.07549 pv × ~9 pips net = ~$0.07` — that's **7 cents per close**.  
Even an L1+L2 basket (2.50 lots): `2.50 × 0.07549 × 9 = ~$1.70`.

The ~$591 Aug 5 loss requires approximately **8,400 L1-only closes** or **350 L1+L2 closes** to recover. With only 202 total TP closes across the year, the math cannot work.

This is the core tension of the conservative fixed-lot setup:
> **Small lot = survives bad events. But surviving a bad event ≠ recovering from it.**

## My Thoughts on "Survived but Still Negative"

**The EMA200 gate is a genuine improvement** — it cut the loss by 70% and kept the account alive all year. The mechanism is clear: AUDCAD spent most of 2024 above its 200-day average, so EMA200 correctly said "this is a bullish trend, don't short." That single decision blocked the fatal Sep basket.

**But it cannot fix the underlying problem**: the Jul 18 LONG entry is genuinely aligned with the long-term trend. EMA200 can't block it because EMA200 says "trend is bullish — long OK." What it can't detect is that a legitimately bullish trend can still have a 3-week, 319-pip counter-trend move that kills a 10-leg grid.

**Three ways to read the EMA200 result:**

1. **As a gate improvement**: EMA200 is a better macro filter than EMA20 for distinguishing trend-aligned from counter-trend baskets. Worth keeping as an additional layer.

2. **As a partial fix only**: Still -11.53%. The remaining emergency (the Jul 18 LONG) is the *same basket* that has emergency'd across every version tested. No gate change touches it because the long-term trend correctly says "long allowed."

3. **The real fix remains structural (Max_Level=6)**: At Max_Level=6, the Jul 18 basket reaches L6 at ~$24 lots. At the Aug 5 low, that's ~20% DD — the basket survives, holds 31 days, closes at TP. **This is the only mechanism that has mathematically proven it can absorb the exact Jul-Aug 2024 move.**

**The path forward**:  
Run `Min_Conf=4 + Max_Level=6 + EMA200` together. The winning combo prevents the emergency structurally; EMA200 as an additional layer should reduce Sep-style counter-trend entries in other years. Both filters are additive, not alternatives.
