## ATR Pause Activity (H1)

Only **5 actual pause activations** across the entire year, compared to 62 on M15:

| # | Pause activated | Resumed | Duration | Basket context |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | May 16 05:00 (ratio=1.84, 17.2 pips) | May 16 10:30 (ratio=1.19) | ~5.5 hours | LONG basket open, no fatal outcome |
| 2 | Jun 13 03:00 (ratio=1.84, 17.0 pips) | Jun 13 12:30 (ratio=1.16) | ~9.5 hours | SHORT basket open, no fatal outcome |
| 3 | **Aug 5 11:00 (ratio=1.89, 28.5 pips)** | Aug 6 07:30 (ratio=1.13) | ~20 hours | **2 HOURS AFTER the emergency at 09:02 — too late** |
| 4 | Sep 19 07:00 (ratio=1.80, 18.8 pips) | Sep 20 00:30 (ratio=1.07) | ~17.5 hours | Sep emergency SHORT basket, L7 delayed by ~15 hours |
| 5 | Nov 6 11:00 (ratio=1.85, 25.0 pips) | Nov 7 07:30 (ratio=1.17) | ~20.5 hours | Post-freeze period, no active basket |

### Why H1 ATR fired only 5 times (vs 62 on M15)

H1 bars smooth out M15 spikes. A single violent M15 candle barely moves the H1 ATR. H1 ATR(14)/ATR(100) only exceeds 1.8 when an **entire trading session** (multiple hours) is running hot — a US NFP release, a central bank surprise, or a risk-off shock. Routine intraday volatility stays invisible.

### Why H1 ATR completely missed the fatal period

On July 25 (the most critical date for the LONG basket), `atr_ratio=1.16` and `atr_paused=0`. The July 2024 AUDCAD decline was a **slow, multi-week grind** — each individual H1 bar looked normal by volatility standards. The H1 ATR never detected it.

| Date | H1 atr_ratio | atr_paused | What happened |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jul 25 00:00–01:30 | 1.16–1.17 | 0 | L6 added freely, L7 added freely |
| Jul 31 04:45 | ~1.0 (calm) | 0 | L8 added freely |
| Aug 5 09:00–09:02 | ~1.0 | 0 | L9 added → **EMERGENCY** |
| Aug 5 11:00 | 1.89 | 1 | H1 finally reacts — **2 hours too late** |

The H1 ATR only "woke up" **after** the market crashed hard enough to produce a spike H1 candle — which happened post-emergency.

## Definitive Conclusion: ATR Pause Cannot Fix the 2024 Problem

The 2024 failure mode is **directional drift**, not volatility:
- A 319-pip decline over 18 days looks "calm" to any ATR measure on any timeframe
- The individual M15 and H1 bars along the way are unremarkable
- The ATR only spikes after the fact — when the crash is sharp enough to produce a large H1 candle, i.e. **after** the emergency

**ATR pause is correctly designed for a different threat**: a fast intraday spike that would force a premature leg addition during a temporary extreme move. That threat does exist in AUDCAD, but it is not what produced the 2024 emergencies.

**The correct structural fix for 2024 remains `Max_Level=6`**, which caps the maximum basket exposure at 24.10 lots. At that size, the 2024 fatal move (−146 pips) only hits ~20% DD — well under the 35% cap. The basket survives and closes 31 days later at TP. No ATR feature, of any timeframe, replicates this structural protection.
