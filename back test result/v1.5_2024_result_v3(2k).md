## The Jul 16 → Aug 16 basket — survived the move that killed every prior run

The same Jul 16 11:45 LONG L1 that fired in v2 (4-arm unanimous BUY at 0.92216) **also fired in v3**. With Max_Level=6, the basket capped at 6 legs and the cap mathematically couldn't fire on a 330-pip drop.

| Leg | Date/Time | Price | Lot | Δ from L1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Jul 16 11:45 | 0.92216 | 0.10 | — |
| L2 | Jul 18 21:45 | 0.91923 | 2.40 | −29.3 pips |
| L3 | Jul 22 05:00 | 0.91614 | 3.60 | −60.2 pips |
| L4 | Jul 22 19:15 | 0.91295 | 4.80 | −92.1 pips |
| L5 | Jul 24 06:15 | 0.91016 | 6.00 | −120.0 pips |
| **L6** | **Jul 25 03:30** | **0.90713** | **7.20** | **−150.3 pips** (cap reached) |
| (price continues falling to 0.88937 on Aug 5 — 326 pips below L1) | | | | |
| **CLOSE** | **Aug 16 22:00** | **0.91291** | **24.10** | **+12.5 pips net** ✅ |

**At the absolute low (Aug 5 @ 0.88937)** — the price where v1 emergency-closed at −146 pips and v2 emergency-closed at −142 pips — this basket was floating at:

- Distance from wavg (~0.91165): **−222.8 pips below wavg**
- Floating loss: `24.10 × 0.07549 × 222.8 ≈ $405`
- DD on $2k: **20.3%** — well under the 35% cap

Then price recovered slowly over the next 11 days, crossed wavg+10 pips on Aug 16, basket closed at TP. The cap **mathematically could not fire** because 24.10 lots × pv × pips can't reach $700 (35% of $2k) unless price moves ~385 pips below wavg, which would mean ~490 pips below L1 — bigger than AUDCAD's actual 330-pip drop.

**This is the structural fix.** Lower max-level → smaller total exposure → cap can't fire on real-world AUDCAD moves.

## What changed from v2 to v3 — math vs reality

**Prediction (v2 md)**: "Re-test 2024 with `Grid_Max_Level_To_SL=6` keeping everything else the same. Pure prediction: emergency losses ~70% smaller, account ends in better shape."

**Reality**: emergency loss isn't 70% smaller — **it's zero**. The cap doesn't fire at all because 6 legs × 30 pips can't accumulate enough lot-pips for floating loss to hit $700 on this scale of move. Better than predicted.

The 4 levers in interaction:

| Lever | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| **$2k capital** | Floats the FitCheck floor; basket can sit through 20%+ floating DD without freezing |
| **Min_Conf=4** | Blocks borderline 3-arm setups (e.g., Sep 11 SHORT entry that emergency'd v1) |
| **Grid_Step=30** | Spreads legs wider so 6 legs cover 150 pips of adverse move before capping |
| **Max_Level=6** | Caps total exposure at 24.10 lots — small enough that even a 330-pip move past L1 only produces ~20% floating DD, well under 35% emergency cap |

Drop any one of these and the configuration starts to leak. Min_Conf=3 with Lv=6 might let too many borderline baskets stack; $1k capital with Lv=6 might still freeze on the FitCheck. The combination is what wins.
