## Why "more capital + wider grid" wasn't enough

The setup successfully absorbed adverse moves up to **~225 pips** (Sep SHORT L8 before emergency at L9 attempt). But 2024 had at least two moves bigger than that:

- **Jul 18 → Aug 5: 319 pips down** (LONG basket trap)
- **Sep 11 → Sep 27: 289 pips up** (SHORT basket trap)

The grid math: with `Grid_Step_Pips=30` and `Grid_Max_Level=10`, the EA caps out at 270 pips of adverse coverage (L1 at 0, L10 at 270 pips away). Above that the cap is the only defense, and the cap fires at 35% equity. Once you've stacked 8–9 legs of 9-multiplier-times-base each, the cap fires regardless.

**Worse**: doubling capital ($1k → $2k) means the 35% emergency cap is now ~$700 instead of ~$350. The deeper "safety budget" lets MORE lots accumulate before the cap fires — so a single emergency loses MORE dollars in absolute terms. That's why the 2024 dollar losses ($583 + $429 = $1012) are far bigger than the 1k v1.4 v2 single emergency loss (~$330).

The percentage loss this run (-39.79%) is **worse** than v1.4 v2's −6.5% on the same year — even though the EA survived more individual adverse moves. The bigger capital = bigger ammunition = bigger bullet wounds when the cap finally fires.

## What would actually have worked on 2024?

Given the data we now have, none of the v1.5 inputs alone fix this. Plausible candidates that aren't tested yet:

1. **Even wider grid (44–60 pips)** — would cut leg accumulation in the 200–300 pip moves. Trade-off: even fewer probes overall, much slower TP recovery on bounces. Worth testing.
2. **Lower `Grid_Max_Level_To_SL` (e.g. 6)** — forces emergency to fire shallower with smaller lots accumulated. The Aug 5 emergency at L6 instead of L9 would have lost ~$130 instead of $584.
3. **`Min_Confluence_Count = 4`** — Jul 18 10:30 had `RSI=46.8` (not ≤40), so unanimous rule would have blocked it. Sep 11 SHORT entry also worth checking. Trade-off: probably blocks 80%+ of probes year-round.
4. **HTF gate redesign (v1.6 territory)** — D1 EMA20 said `gate_long=1` throughout the Jul→Aug drop and `gate_short=1` throughout the Sep rise, both of which were *correct directional reads* — gate didn't fail. The problem is the gate doesn't prevent **continuing** to open new probes during a multi-day move. A "no-new-probe within X hours of basket close" lockout might help, or a "wait for first counter-move before re-entering" filter.

## Verdict

The "$2k + step=30 + fixed 0.10 + Min_Conf=3" configuration:

- **Works on easy years** (2025 +15.66%, 0 emergencies)
- **Improves on hard months where v1.4 1k failed** (April 2024 deep baskets all closed green)
- **But does NOT survive the worst 2024 moves** (2 emergencies, frozen Q4, −39.79% net)

This is honest progress over v1.4 in terms of *what it can absorb*, but it's traded freeze-after-1-emergency at $1k for bigger-dollar-losses-and-freeze-after-2-emergencies at $2k. The fundamental issue — that a 300-pip directional move with the HTF gate aligned wrong will always trigger the cap on a 10-leg grid — is unsolved.

**Next priorities** (in order of expected impact):
1. Re-test 2024 with `Grid_Max_Level_To_SL=6` keeping everything else the same. Pure prediction: emergency losses ~70% smaller, account ends in better shape.
2. Re-test 2024 with `Min_Confluence_Count=4`. Pure prediction: probably blocks the Jul 18 basket entirely.
3. Replay 2023 at this exact setup ($2k step=30) — was an even worse year for 1k runs (2 emergencies in June).
