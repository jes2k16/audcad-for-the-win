## ⚠️ CRITICAL CAVEAT — wc_pct understates the real 2024 risk

The wc_pct=12.67% looks safely under the 35% cap, and 2025 had zero emergencies. **But this does NOT mean 0.30 is safe on 2024.** Here's the trap:

**`wc_pct` measures the DD if price stops at the bottom of the 6-leg ladder (L6, ~150 pips below L1).** It does NOT account for price continuing *past* L6. On a sustained move, once all 6 legs are filled, the basket floats with NO new legs — and the DD keeps growing linearly as price falls further.

The 2024 Jul-Aug fatal move went **326 pips below L1** — that's **176 pips past L6**. At that extension, the actual DD is far above wc_pct.

## The corrected safe-lot derivation

My earlier "keep wc_pct under 15%" heuristic was **wrong** — it relied on wc_pct, which understates deep-move risk. The correct anchor is: **what DD does a 2024-style 326-pip move produce?**

From the v3 proof: at base 0.10, the worst 2024 basket floated at **20.3% DD** at its lowest point.

DD scales linearly with base lot, so for the basket to stay under the 35% cap on a 2024-style move:

```
base_max = 0.10 × (35% / 20.3%) = 0.172
```

**The maximum fixed lot that survives a 2024-style move at $2k is ~0.15–0.17, not 0.30.**

| Base lot | 2024 worst-basket DD (projected) | 2024 verdict |
|---|---:|:---:|
| 0.10 (v3 proven) | 20.3% | ✅ survives |
| **0.15** | ~30% | ✅ survives (thin margin) |
| 0.17 | ~35% | ⚠️ right at the cap |
| **0.30 (v5)** | **~61%** | ❌ **emergencies** |
| auto 0.76 | >100% pre-cap | ❌ emergencies |

## Verdict

v5 confirms the sizing dial is smooth and predictable on calm years: **0.30 fixed = +20.57%, ~3× the v3 return.** But the 2024 survival math shows **0.30 is above the safe threshold** — it would likely emergency on a 2024-style sustained move (~61% DD at the Aug 5 low).

**The honest safe ceiling for a fixed lot at $2k is ~0.15.** That captures ~50% more return than v3 (~+10–11% on 2025, projected) while keeping the worst 2024 basket under the 35% cap.

### Recommended next tests (in priority order)

1. **Run v5 (0.30) on 2024** to confirm or refute the projected emergency. If it emergencies as predicted, 0.30 is rejected.
2. **Run fixed 0.15 on BOTH 2024 and 2025.** This is the projected true safe-maximum: survives 2024 (~30% DD at worst), ~1.5× the v3 return on 2025. If 2024 stays clean, **0.15 becomes the recommended production lot** — best return that's still structurally bounded.

The principle going forward: **size the fixed lot off the worst historical DD (the 2024 Jul-Aug 326-pip move), not off wc_pct.** wc_pct is a FitCheck convenience that assumes price stops at the ladder bottom — real markets don't.
