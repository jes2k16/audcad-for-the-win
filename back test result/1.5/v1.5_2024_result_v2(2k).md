| Arm | Reading | BUY threshold | Vote? |
|---|---:|---|:---:|
| StochRSI %K | 18.2 | ≤ 20 | ✅ |
| Bollinger %B | 0.05 | ≤ 0.10 | ✅ |
| RSI(14) | 33.4 | ≤ 40 | ✅ |
| dist to swing low | 47 pips | ≤ 50 | ✅ |

## Why v2 ended much better despite the same emergency

The single Aug 5 emergency was almost identical in magnitude to v1's Aug 5 emergency. What changed:

1. **v1 had a SECOND emergency in Sep** (−$429) → v2 prevented it via Min_Conf=4 blocking the Sep 11 SHORT
2. **v1 dropped below FitCheck floor after Sep** → 758 SKIP_PROBE events, account frozen Oct–Dec
3. **v2 didn't have the second emergency** → equity stayed high enough for fixed-0.10 to fit the 10-leg ladder → kept trading all of Q4

Q4 activity in v2: 6 closes Oct, **22 closes Nov**, 9 closes Dec — the EA had its busiest month of the year in November. That's where most of the $406 recovery vs v1 came from.

## What this experiment definitively proves

1. **Unanimous confluence (`Min_Conf=4`) works on borderline 3-arm setups** — both v1 fatals (Jul 18 LONG, Sep 11 SHORT) were borderline cases (one dissenting arm just past threshold), and v2 cleanly blocked both.

2. **Unanimous confluence does NOT save you from genuine 4-arm oversold/overbought setups.** The Jul 16 11:45 LONG had every indicator in deep agreement — and was still ~330 pips early on the move.

3. **The 270-pip ladder coverage wall is still the real limit.** No signal-side change fixes it because the signal is *correctly* identifying oversold conditions. The market is just going further than oversold-by-indicators implies.

4. **Avoiding the second emergency matters enormously for survival.** v1 → v2 turned `-39.79%` into `-19.46%` mostly because Q4 stayed alive. The Aug 5 hit hurts ~$570 in both cases; what differs is whether the account can recover.

## The lever stack now ranked by empirical impact (post v2)

| Lever | What it fixes | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **More capital ($1k → $2k)** | Floats the freeze floor; basket survives moderate adverse moves | Bigger $ loss per emergency (cap is %) |
| **Wider grid (22 → 30)** | Fewer legs per 100-pip move; absorbs ≤225-pip swings | Slower TP recovery on bounces; doesn't help 300+ pip moves |
| **Min_Conf=4** | Blocks borderline 3-arm setups (Sep 11 SHORT prevented in 2024) | Doesn't block genuine 4-arm agreement; may move L1 earlier in the move; ~18% fewer probes |
| **Untested: Max_Level=6** | Forces cap to fire shallower → much smaller $ loss per emergency | Many more emergencies overall (more baskets reach the cap) |
| **Untested: HTF gate redesign (v1.6)** | Could block re-entries during multi-day moves | Bigger architectural change |

---

## Verdict

`Min_Confluence_Count=4` is a **net positive** for 2024 (+20 pp vs Min_Conf=3 baseline) and unambiguously prevents the second-emergency-then-freeze trap that defines the 2024 1k runs. It's the cheapest lever we've found that meaningfully improves the bad-year outcome.

But it doesn't break the **270-pip ladder coverage wall**. A genuine 4-arm oversold reading at the start of a 330-pip move is still a fatal basket — just one with no second-emergency follow-up.

**Next step**: combine Min_Conf=4 with `Grid_Max_Level_To_SL=6`. Prediction: the Aug 5 emergency fires at L6 instead of L9 — ~$120 loss instead of $570 — pulling the 2024 result close to break-even or positive.
