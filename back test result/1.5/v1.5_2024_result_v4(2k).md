## The critical lesson — Max_Level is NOT a safety lever when auto-sizing is ON

This is the most important finding of the whole v4 experiment:

> **The auto-sizer computes the base lot to consume the full 35% DD budget across whatever ladder it's given. So lowering `Grid_Max_Level_To_SL` doesn't reduce risk under auto-sizing — the auto-sizer just makes each leg bigger to hit the same 35% target.**

Evidence: `wc_pct = 34.87%` at Max_Level=6 in v4, vs `wc_pct = 4.59%` at the same Max_Level=6 in v3. Same max-level, same grid step — but auto-sizing pinned the worst-case at the cap, while fixed 0.10 left it at 4.59%.

| Lever | Effect on risk under FIXED lot | Effect on risk under AUTO lot |
|---|---|---|
| Lower `Max_Level` (10→6) | **Big** — caps total exposure, makes cap unreachable | **None** — auto-sizer inflates per-leg lot to compensate |
| Lower `Max_Drawdown_Percentage` | Moderate | **The only lever** — directly lowers the auto-sizer's target |
| Smaller fixed lot | **The main lever** | n/a (auto overrides) |

**Under auto-sizing, the cap is always armed at ~`Max_Drawdown_Percentage`% by design.** That's the auto-sizer's entire job — spend the budget. So auto-sizing + any Max_Level = emergency risk on deep baskets.

## One thing auto-sizing did better — no freeze

v4 had **0 SKIP_PROBE events** — it never froze, unlike the fixed-lot $1k runs and the v1.5_2024_2k v1 (which had 758 skips in Q4). Why: after each emergency, the auto-sizer recomputed a smaller base from the reduced equity (base dropped from 0.76 → 0.37 over the year), so probes always "fit" the FitCheck. The account kept trading and clawed back from the post-emergency lows.

That's the one structural advantage of auto-sizing: **it self-adjusts down and never permanently locks out.** But it pays for that by emergency-ing repeatedly instead.

Base lot trajectory: 0.76 (Jan) → dropped to 0.37 after the April double-emergency → recovered toward 0.87 as equity rebuilt. November had 22 closes (the recovery month, same as every other run).

## The complete 2×2 — sizing vs year

We now have all four cells of the experiment:

| | **2024 (hard year)** | **2025 (easy year)** |
|---|---:|---:|
| **v3 (fixed 0.10, wc 4%)** | **+7.96%** ✅ 0 emerg | **+6.86%** ✅ 0 emerg |
| **v4 (auto, wc 35%)** | **−37.22%** ❌ 3 emerg | **+75.03%** 🚀 0 emerg |

The pattern is now unambiguous:

- **Fixed small lot (v3)**: consistent, boring, positive both years, structurally cannot blow up. Low return.
- **Auto-sizing (v4)**: spectacular on easy years (+75%), catastrophic on hard years (−37%). The cap is always armed.

**Auto-sizing is a leveraged bet on the year being calm.** 2025 was calm → +75%. 2024 was not → −37%. You cannot know in advance which kind of year you'll get.

## Verdict — auto-sizing is rejected for production

The v4 experiment definitively answers the profit question and closes the auto-sizing path:

1. **Auto-sizing delivers the big returns** (+75% on 2025) — the profit was never a strategy problem, just a sizing choice.
2. **But auto-sizing re-arms the emergency cap** (wc_pct 35%) regardless of Max_Level, because the auto-sizer's job is to spend the full DD budget.
3. **On a hard year (2024) the armed cap fires repeatedly** — 3 emergencies, −37.22%, turning even April's winning baskets into losses.
4. **Max_Level=6 provides zero protection under auto-sizing** — the safety in v3 came from the small fixed lot, full stop.

**The only configuration in the test set that survives both years is v3: fixed 0.10 lot, Max_Level=6, Min_Conf=4, wc_pct=4%.** It returns little (~7% / year) but cannot structurally blow up.

## The path forward — middle-ground fixed lot

If +6.86% is too small but auto-sizing's blow-up risk is unacceptable, the answer is a **fixed lot bigger than 0.10 but small enough to keep wc_pct well under the cap**:

| Fixed base | Est. wc_pct ($2k) | Cap status | Expected return profile |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 0.10 (v3) | ~4.6% | unreachable | +7% (proven safe) |
| **0.20** | ~9% | unreachable | ~+14% (likely safe — test) |
| **0.30** | ~14% | unreachable | ~+21% (likely safe — test) |
| 0.50 | ~23% | getting close | risky on deep baskets |
| 0.76+ (auto) | ~35% | **armed** | blows up on hard years |

**Recommended next test**: fixed `Default_Base_Lot_Size = 0.20` or `0.30` (auto OFF), Max_Level=6, Min_Conf=4, on BOTH 2024 and 2025. This scales returns 2–3× above v3 while keeping the cap unreachable. The goal is the highest fixed lot whose wc_pct stays under ~15% — capturing more of the upside without re-arming the cap.
