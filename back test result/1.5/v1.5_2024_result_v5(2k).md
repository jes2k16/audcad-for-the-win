## Correcting the v5-2025 analysis

The v5-2025 md projected "~−35%" for 2024 and called 0.30 "above the safe threshold." **That was too pessimistic.** The correction:

- **What was right**: 0.30 *does* emergency on the 2024 July basket (predicted correctly).
- **What was wrong**: the *magnitude*. The emergency cap limits each event to 35% of equity at firing time, and at $2k the account recovers instead of freezing. One capped emergency in an otherwise-winning year nets only −6.35%, not −35%.
- **The corrected "safe lot" framing**: there isn't a hard safe/unsafe cliff at 0.17. It's a variance spectrum. 0.10 = zero emergencies; 0.30 = occasional capped emergency with recovery; auto = frequent emergencies. All survive at $2k.

The "size off worst historical DD" principle still holds for *avoiding emergencies entirely* (≤0.15-0.17). But 0.30 shows that *tolerating* a capped emergency is also viable when capital is high enough to avoid the freeze.

## Verdict — 0.30 is a legitimate higher-variance option

v5 reframes the choice. It's not "v3 safe vs everything-else-blows-up." It's a **variance spectrum, all survivable at $2k**:

| If you want… | Use | 2024 / 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest variance, never a down year | **0.10 (v3)** | +7.96% / +6.86% |
| Higher returns, accept occasional mild down years | **0.30 (v5)** | −6.35% / +20.57% |
| Max returns, accept big swings | auto (v4) | −37.22% / +75.03% |

**Recommendation depends on risk tolerance:**
- Conservative deployment → **0.10** (proven steady).
- Growth-oriented, can stomach a −6% year → **0.30** is viable (one capped emergency, recovers, no freeze, 3× the calm-year return).
- **0.15-0.20** remains worth testing as the sweet spot — likely zero emergencies (like 0.10) with ~50-100% more return.
