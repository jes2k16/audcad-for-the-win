## The critical caveat — v4 is UNTESTED on a hard year

**2025's +75% does not prove v4 is safe.** It only proves that *when no basket gets deep*, auto-sizing massively outperforms fixed sizing. 2025's deepest basket was L4 — the armed cap was never tested.

The real question: **does v4 (auto-sizing, wc_pct=35%) survive 2024?**

Strong prior that it does NOT:
- The 2024 Jul-Aug fatal basket reached L6 (in v3 it survived at 20.3% DD because the *fixed 0.10* lots were tiny).
- With auto-sizing at wc_pct=35%, that same L6 basket would be consuming the full 35% budget at full grid extension. The Aug 5 low (326 pips below L1) would push it **past** the cap → emergency.
- Essentially v4 on 2024 would behave like v1.5_2024_2k v1 (Min_Conf=3, fixed but bigger effective size) which lost −39.79%, or worse.

**v3's safety was structural (wc_pct=4%); v4's safety on 2025 was circumstantial (no deep basket).** Do not deploy v4 until it's run on 2024 and 2023.

## Verdict

v4 answers the profit question definitively: **the v1.5 safe structure (Min_Conf=4 + Max_Level=6) is fully compatible with auto-sizing, and auto-sizing turns the same trades into +75% via compounding.**

BUT — auto-sizing re-arms the emergency cap (wc_pct 4% → 35%). The +75% is real but came on an easy year where the cap was never tested. **This is NOT yet a safe production config — it's the high-return/high-risk version of v3.**

The decision tree:
- **Want bulletproof survival, accept small returns** → v3 (fixed 0.10, wc_pct 4%, +6.86% on 2025, +7.96% on 2024, cap unreachable)
- **Want big returns, accept blow-up risk** → v4 (auto, wc_pct 35%, +75% on 2025, UNTESTED and likely fatal on 2024)

**Next step (mandatory before any conclusion)**: run v4 config (auto-sizing + Min_Conf=4 + Max_Level=6) on **2024** and **2023**. Prediction: emergency fires on both, likely freezing the account — confirming that auto-sizing's compounding upside is inseparable from the emergency risk that v3's fixed tiny lot was specifically designed to remove.

The middle ground worth exploring: a **fixed lot between 0.10 and 0.82** (e.g. 0.20–0.30) that lifts returns above v3's +6.86% while keeping wc_pct well under the cap (~8–12%) so the cap stays unreachable. That's the "more profit without re-arming the bomb" path.
